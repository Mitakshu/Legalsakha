Short, plain-language explainers on the basics. Rules change, so confirm details with our team.

🏢

Pvt Ltd or LLP?

Both give limited liability. Pvt Ltd is often chosen when raising investment, while an LLP can suit professional and smaller partnerships.

™️

Why register a trademark?

Registration gives your brand name or logo legal protection and a stronger position if someone copies it.

📑

GST basics

GST is the indirect tax on supply of goods and services. Registration requirements depend on turnover, type of supply and state.

🏛️

What is ROC filing?

Companies and LLPs must file periodic returns and financial statements with the Registrar of Companies.

📒

Why bookkeeping matters

Clean books make tax filing, audits, loans and decisions much easier.

🚢

What is an IEC?

Import Export Code is a unique code generally needed to import or export from India.
